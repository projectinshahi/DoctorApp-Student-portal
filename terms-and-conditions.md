### 1. One account, one person

Your email address identifies your account, and only one device can be signed
in at a time. Signing in somewhere new ends the earlier session, and that
device is shown a message explaining why. Keep your password to yourself —
anything done through your account is treated as done by you.

account, you are signed into that same account rather than a new one.

### 2. Free and premium content

Some courses, videos and quizzes are free; the rest need an active
subscription. Individual lessons inside a premium course may be marked as free
previews.

When a subscription expires, premium content locks again — but your progress,
scores and quiz history stay exactly where you left them, ready for when you
renew.

### 3. Subscriptions and payment

Monthly and yearly plans are paid through Razorpay, and a plan starts once the
payment is confirmed. If a payment fails or is abandoned, no subscription is
created and you can simply try again.

Renewal is yours to start — nothing is billed automatically and no card is
kept on file. Renewing before expiry extends your existing term; renewing
after expiry starts a fresh term from the payment date.

Refunds are handled through the payment gateway, outside the app.

### 4. How quizzes are scored

The quiz timer runs on our server, so closing the app or losing signal does not
pause it. When you come back you resume the same attempt with the time that is
left, and the attempt submits itself when the time runs out, with whatever
answers were recorded.

- One option may be selected per question, and you may change it any time
  before you submit.
- A question left unattempted scores zero and carries no negative marking.
- The number of attempts allowed is set per quiz and shown before you start.
- Only your best attempt on a quiz counts towards the leaderboard, so
  re-attempting cannot inflate a rank.

Please do not use unauthorised help or any method of manipulating a result.

### 5. Video lessons

Videos are streamed through secure, expiring links and are never downloadable.
A lesson is marked complete once you have watched 90% of it, and your last
position is remembered so you can pick it up again on any device.
