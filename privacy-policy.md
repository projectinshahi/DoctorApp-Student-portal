# Privacy Policy

**Dr. SKM's Academy**

Effective date: `[Date]`
Last updated: `[Date]`

This policy explains what Dr. SKM's Academy stores about you, why it stores it,
and who else ever sees it. It is short on purpose — a policy you can finish
reading is worth more than one that covers every hypothetical.

---

### 1. Your account details

Your email address, password, display name, contact number and profile photo.

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address on that
account and use it to identify you. An existing account with that address is
linked, not duplicated.

### 2. What you do while learning

- Lessons and courses you open
- How far into each video you have watched, and where you stopped
- Course and chapter completion
- Quiz attempts, answers, scores and percentages
- Subject-wise performance and leaderboard rank
- Comments and replies you post

This is what makes progress tracking, continue-learning, quiz history,
recommendations and the leaderboard work. Recommendations are generated from
your own activity using fixed, rule-based logic — same subject first, then your
lowest-scoring topic, then whatever was published most recently.

### 3. Your subscription

Which plan you are on, when it starts and expires, and the reference and status
of each payment, whether it succeeded or failed.

Card and bank details go straight to Razorpay. They never reach us and we never
store them. Razorpay handles that information under its own privacy policy.

### 4. Email we send you

Four kinds only:

1. Registration confirmation
2. Password reset link
3. Payment confirmation
4. A reminder before your subscription expires

No marketing email, no SMS, no WhatsApp.

### 5. Who else sees it

Only the services that run the platform:

- Sign-in providers (Google, Apple)
- Razorpay, the payment gateway
- The cloud account that hosts and streams video
- Our email delivery provider

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

### 6. Comments are public

A comment you post on a video lesson is visible to other students along with
your display name, so please keep personal or confidential details out of it.
You can edit or delete your own at any time. A hidden or deleted comment
disappears from the student view along with its replies.

### 7. How it is protected

Video plays through expiring links that cannot be shared, only one device can
be signed in to an account at a time, access to premium content is checked at
the point of delivery rather than only in the interface, and screenshots and
screen recording are blocked in the mobile app.

No system online is completely secure, and we do not claim otherwise.

### 8. How long we keep it

For as long as your account exists. A deactivated account loses access
immediately while its progress, scores and payment records are retained — and
when a course is archived or unpublished, the progress and attempts recorded
against it are kept too.

There is no self-service delete in the app yet. Email us at the address below
and we will handle the request.

### 9. Younger students, and changes

This is an educational app. A student below the age of consent in their country
should use it with a parent or guardian involved.

The web version may use cookies where they are needed for sign-in, session
management and security. If analytics or advertising technology is ever
introduced, this policy will be updated first.

The current version of this policy always lives in the app under
Profile → Privacy Policy.

**By creating an account or continuing to use Dr. SKM's Academy, you agree to
your information being handled as described here.**
